# how to deposit on OKX: a step-by-step guide for crypto transfers, cash deposits, networks, fees, and common mistakes

If you are searching for **how to deposit on OKX**, you probably want to move funds into your account without sending crypto to the wrong network, missing a memo, or waiting several hours while wondering whether the transaction disappeared into the blockchain.

The basic process is straightforward:

1. Complete identity verification.
2. Open the **Deposit** page in the OKX app or website.
3. Choose the asset and network.
4. Copy the deposit address, and memo or tag if required.
5. Send the funds from your wallet, bank, or another exchange.
6. Wait for the required confirmations.

The details matter, though. A USDT deposit sent through the wrong network can be difficult or impossible to recover. A bank deposit may have different limits and processing times from a crypto transfer. The options shown in your account also depend on your location, verification level, selected currency, and the OKX entity serving your region.

This guide explains how to deposit crypto and cash on OKX, what to check before sending funds, how fees work, and what to do when a deposit does not arrive.

## Before you deposit on OKX

You need an OKX account that has completed the required identity verification before making a deposit. Accounts that are frozen, restricted, or missing required verification may have limited deposit functionality.

You should also check whether OKX supports the asset and service in your region. Not every cryptocurrency, network, cash currency, or payment method is available everywhere. The deposit screen is the most useful place to confirm what your account can currently receive.

Before sending any money, prepare the following:

- The cryptocurrency or cash currency you plan to deposit
- The sending wallet, bank account, or other exchange
- The correct deposit network
- The required memo, tag, reference number, or payment details
- Enough funds to cover any network or payment-provider fees
- A small test amount if you are using a network or platform for the first time

For new users, a small test deposit is usually worth the extra few minutes. It is much cheaper than learning that you copied a Polygon address while the sending platform was configured for Ethereum.

## How to deposit crypto on the OKX app

The mobile process is similar across supported cryptocurrencies.

### 1. Open the Deposit page

Open the OKX app and choose **Assets**, then **Deposit**. Depending on the version of the app and your region, you may also see a **Transfer** or **Deposit** button directly on the home screen. Choose the option for depositing crypto, not cash.

### 2. Select the cryptocurrency

Search for the asset you want to deposit, such as BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, XRP, or another supported token.

Do not select an asset only because the ticker looks familiar. Some tokens exist on several blockchains, and the same ticker can appear with different network options. Check the exact asset name and available network list shown by OKX.

### 3. Choose the deposit network

Select the network that matches the withdrawal network on the platform sending your funds.

For example, if you choose USDT on the Ethereum network in OKX, the sending platform must also send USDT through Ethereum. If the sending platform is set to Tron, Solana, Arbitrum, or another network, you must select that same supported network on OKX.

The asset and network must match on both sides:

| OKX deposit selection | Sending platform selection |
| --- | --- |
| USDT on Ethereum | USDT on Ethereum |
| USDT on Tron | USDT on Tron |
| ETH on Ethereum | ETH on Ethereum |
| USDC on Arbitrum | USDC on Arbitrum |

A matching ticker alone is not enough. **USDT is not one single blockchain asset for transfer purposes.** It can be issued or supported across multiple networks, and the wrong combination can result in a missing or delayed deposit.

### 4. Copy the address and memo or tag

After choosing the asset and network, OKX displays the deposit address. Some assets also require a tag, memo, comment, or reference number.

For example, certain exchange-based assets such as XRP may require both an address and a destination tag. If OKX displays a memo or tag, copy it together with the address. Sending only the address may prevent the deposit from being credited automatically.

Use the app’s copy function where possible. Manually typing a blockchain address is an unnecessary invitation to make a very expensive typo.

### 5. Start the withdrawal from the sending platform

Open the wallet, exchange, or payment platform where your crypto currently sits. Choose **Withdraw** or **Send**, then paste the OKX deposit address.

Select the same network you selected on OKX. Enter the memo, tag, or comment if one is required. Review the amount, network fee, destination address, and estimated arrival time before confirming.

### 6. Wait for blockchain confirmations

A crypto deposit may not appear instantly. OKX generally credits the funds after the required blockchain confirmations have been completed. The number of confirmations and the minimum deposit amount can vary by asset and are displayed on the deposit page.

If the amount sent is below the minimum shown by OKX, the deposit may not be credited. That is why checking the deposit page before sending matters, especially for small transfers.

## How to deposit crypto on the OKX website

The website follows the same logic as the mobile app.

2. Go to **Assets** and choose **Deposit**.
3. Select the cryptocurrency.
4. Select the deposit network.
5. Choose the funding or trading account where available.

OKX allows users to choose whether the deposit goes to a Funding or Trading account when that option is available. The exact account layout can vary by product and region, so check the destination before submitting the transfer.

The QR code option can be convenient when sending funds from a mobile wallet. It reduces manual copying, but you should still verify the asset and network on the sending platform before confirming.

## How to deposit cash or fiat currency on OKX

A cash deposit is different from a crypto deposit. Instead of sending a token to a blockchain address, you transfer money through a supported bank or payment method.

On the OKX app, the general path is:

1. Select **Transfer** from the home page.
2. Choose **Deposit**.
3. Select the available cash currency.
4. Choose one of the listed deposit methods.
5. Review the processing fee, limits, and estimated processing time.
6. Follow the payment instructions.
7. Confirm the deposit after checking the preview screen.

On the website, you can usually begin from **Deposit**, choose **Deposit cash**, or open **Assets** and select **Deposit**. The available currencies and payment methods are determined by your region and account.

For US users, OKX states that supported cash deposit options and currencies are shown after signing in. The deposit page displays the methods available to the specific account rather than providing one universal list for every customer. Some products and services may also be restricted depending on the user’s state or location.

### Cash deposit fees

Cash deposit fees are method-specific. If a processing fee applies, OKX displays the amount when you select the payment method and again on the deposit preview screen. If no processing fee is shown, the page may display that no fee is stated.

That does not necessarily mean every part of the transfer is free. Your bank, card provider, payment processor, or correspondent institution may apply separate charges. Check the complete cost before submitting the payment.

### Cash deposit limits

Cash deposit limits may include:

- Minimum amount per transaction
- Maximum amount per transaction
- Daily limit
- Monthly limit
- Remaining limit for the selected method
- Account-level limits based on verification

OKX notes that some limits reset on a rolling basis rather than at midnight. A daily limit can reset 24 hours after a transaction, while a monthly limit may use a rolling 30-day period. The information icon next to the deposit method provides the most relevant limit details for your account.

### Cash deposit processing time

Some cash deposit methods may be instant, while bank transfers can take longer. OKX says certain bank transfers may take up to five business days depending on the transfer type and bank. The estimated processing time is shown when you select the deposit method.

Do not submit a second deposit simply because the first one is still processing. Check your bank confirmation, reference number, and OKX deposit history first.

## Crypto deposit versus cash deposit

The right method depends on where your funds currently are.

| Deposit method | Best for | Main detail to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crypto transfer | Funds already held in another wallet or exchange | Asset and network must match |
| Bank transfer | Moving local currency into OKX | Currency, bank instructions, limits, and processing time |
| Card or payment method | Supported instant or near-instant funding | Availability, processing fee, and regional restrictions |
| Internal OKX transfer | Moving funds between eligible OKX accounts or products | Destination account and transfer instructions |

A crypto deposit requires a blockchain address. A cash deposit requires bank or payment instructions. Mixing up the two workflows is one of the easiest ways to select the wrong option in the app.

## The most common OKX deposit mistakes

### Choosing the wrong network

This is the most serious mistake. Sending USDC through one network to an address intended for another may cause a delayed or unsupported deposit.

Always compare the network selected on OKX with the network selected on the sending platform. Do not assume that two networks are interchangeable because the asset name is the same.

### Forgetting a memo or tag

Some assets require an additional identifier beyond the wallet address. If OKX displays a memo, tag, comment, or reference number, include it exactly as shown.

### Sending an unsupported token

If the token or network does not appear as an option on the OKX deposit page, do not send it to an address manually. OKX warns that unsupported deposits may be lost.

### Sending less than the minimum amount

The deposit page shows the minimum deposit amount for supported assets. Sending less than that amount may result in the funds not being credited automatically.

### Using an old address without checking

OKX supports multiple deposit addresses in some cases, but deposit details can change because of account or system updates. Open the current deposit page and confirm the address before each transfer, especially if you have not used the account for a long time.

### Confusing the exchange account with the OKX Wallet

The OKX exchange account and the separate Web3 wallet do not always use the same deposit workflow. If you are depositing to the exchange, use the exchange’s **Deposit** function under your account assets. If you are transferring to a self-custody wallet, verify the wallet network and address separately.

## What to do if your OKX deposit is missing

Start with the transaction status on the sending platform.

### If the withdrawal is still pending

The sending platform may not have broadcast the transaction yet. Wait for it to move from pending to completed, then check the transaction ID.

### If the transaction is confirmed on-chain

Copy the transaction hash and review it on the relevant blockchain explorer. Confirm:

- The destination address matches your OKX deposit address
- The asset matches the selected asset
- The network is correct
- The amount meets OKX’s minimum
- The transaction has enough confirmations

### If the address or network was wrong

Do not send another transaction immediately. Save the transaction hash, sending platform details, asset, network, amount, and timestamp. Contact OKX support and the sending platform as soon as possible.

Recovery is not guaranteed for unsupported assets, incorrect networks, or missing memos. The earlier you provide complete transaction information, the easier it is for support to determine whether any recovery path exists.

### If a cash deposit is delayed

Check the bank transfer status, payment reference, recipient details, and the processing estimate shown by OKX. If the bank marks the transfer as complete but the balance has not updated after the stated processing period, contact OKX support with the bank confirmation and reference number.

## A safer first-deposit checklist

Before pressing **Confirm**, review these items:

- Identity verification is complete.
- The asset is supported in your region.
- The selected network matches the sending platform.
- The deposit address was copied from the current OKX deposit page.
- The memo or tag is included if required.
- The amount is above the displayed minimum.
- The sending platform’s withdrawal fee is understood.
- The destination account is correct.
- A test transaction is being used when the transfer is unfamiliar.
- The transaction ID will be saved after sending.

For cash deposits, replace the network checks with currency, bank details, payment limits, processing fee, and reference-number checks.
